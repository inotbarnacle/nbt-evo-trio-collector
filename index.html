NBT Evo Trio Collector

Help check another firmware version for BoostDeck, ShiftDeck and MHD Maps.

1. Connect to the head unit

Park the car, connect the ENET cable and enable temporary SSH for your firmware. Bavarian SSH Client

If discovery only finds a gateway, enter the head unit SSH IP from your unlock tool. Try defaults checks the reference car's SSH address, 169.254.199.119. Its gateway is 169.254.21.3.

The report includes the firmware code, HMI build when available, framework hashes and exported symbols. The collector uses fixed read-only commands and excludes vehicle identifiers and credentials from the ZIP.

2. Review and download

    Attach the downloaded ZIP to an email in your usual mail app and send it to nordicbmwdealer@gmail.com.
